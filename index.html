Mint

A vocabulary app for iPhone.

Mint helps you learn words in a new language a few at a time. You study from packs of words and example sentences, keep track of what you know, and can put a word on your Home Screen with a widget.

  • Ready-made packs to start from, or packs built around your own goal.
  • Your vocabulary list, progress and settings stay on your device.
  • An account is optional and is used only for the AI features.
  • No advertising and no analytics.
